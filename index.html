4th Step Moral Inventory

Alcoholics Anonymous • Chapter 5: "How It Works" (pp. 64–71) • four.kevns.com
Step 4 Core Process

Resentment Inventory — The Four Columns (pp. 64–67)

"Resentment is the 'number one' offender. It destroys more alcoholics than anything else... In dealing with resentments, we set them on paper. We listed people, institutions or principles with whom we were angry." Disregard what others have done and resolutely search for your own mistakes in Column 4.

Freedom From Fear

Fear Inventory — Reviewing Our Fears (pp. 67–68)

"We reviewed our fears thoroughly. We put them on paper, even though we had no resentment in connection with them. We asked ourselves why we had them. Wasn't it because self-reliance failed us? ... We ask Him to remove our fear and direct our attention to what He would have us be."

Relationship Overhaul

Sex Conduct & Relationships (pp. 68–70)

"Now about sex. Many of us needed an overhauling there. But above all, we tried to be sensible on this subject... We reviewed our own past conduct over the years. We subjected each relation to this test: was it selfish or not? We asked God to mold our ideals and help us to live up to them."

Steps 8 & 9 Foundation

Harms to Others — Preparation for Amends

A comprehensive inventory of people or institutions you have damaged, cheated, lied to, or neglected. This forms the foundation for making a list of all persons we had harmed and becoming willing to make amends to them all.

Spiritual Diagnostics

Defect Patterns & Threatened Instincts Analysis

"Selfishness - self-centeredness! That, we think, is the root of our troubles." Observe the recurring patterns of which instincts (Security, Pride, Pocketbook, Sex, Ambitions) are repeatedly threatened, and which character defects can be transformed into spiritual assets.
